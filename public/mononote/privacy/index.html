Mononote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-13
Last updated: 2026-09-13

Mononote is a single-note app for Android, published by <LEGAL_ENTITY> (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what Mononote does and does not do with your information. It covers the Android app com.oscillator.mononote and nothing else.

The short version

Your note stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server that holds your writing. The only information that ever leaves your device is your purchase history, and only if you buy Mononote Pro.

Your notes

  • Your active note and every archived note are stored only on your device.
  • They are encrypted at rest with a key held in your phone's Android Keystore, hardware-backed on devices that support it. The key is not exportable: the app itself can never read it.
  • They are excluded from Google cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, because the encryption key cannot move between devices. If you change phone, use Export and Import inside the app to carry your notes across.
  • We never receive your notes, so we cannot read them, hand them over, or recover them for you.
  • Mononote is designed to display your note outside the app: on your lock screen, in the notification shade and in a home-screen widget. When you turn those on, Android's own system UI shows the text. Anyone who can see your screen can read it. “Hide note on lock screen” and the biometric app lock are there for when that is not what you want.
  • Exporting or sharing a note sends it to whichever app you choose in the Android share sheet. That transfer is yours, not ours, and what the receiving app does with it is governed by that app's policy.

Purchases

Mononote Pro is a one-time purchase. If you buy it:

  • Google Play Billing processes the payment. We never see your card details, name or address.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. manages the purchase receipt so the app knows you own Pro, including after a reinstall or on a new phone. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when, the store's transaction identifiers, and your country and currency), and an anonymous app-user identifier that we generate. We do not send RevenueCat your name, your email address, your advertising identifier or your notes.
  • Purpose: to deliver what you paid for (app functionality) and to understand aggregate purchase numbers (analytics). Legal basis in the EEA and the UK: performance of the contract you enter when you buy Pro.
  • All of this data is encrypted in transit.

If you never buy anything, Mononote transmits nothing at all.

What we do not collect

No name. No email address. No phone number. No account. No location, precise or approximate. No contacts, calendar, photos, files or messages. No advertising identifier or device identifier. No analytics on how you use the app. No crash reports or diagnostics. No ads and no advertising trackers, ever.

Mononote requests the notification permission only so it can show your note on your lock screen. That permission gives us nothing about you.

How long it is kept

  • Notes: on your device, until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes them.
  • Purchase records: kept by RevenueCat and Google for as long as the entitlement exists, and for as long as they are required to keep transaction records. Deleting them ends your ability to restore Pro without repurchasing.

Deleting your data

  • Notes: delete them in the app, or uninstall Mononote. Nothing is left on a server, because nothing was ever sent to one.
  • Purchase history: email dobrzanskioscillator@gmail.com from the address you would like us to act on, or use https://michaldanieldobrzanski.github.io/mononote/delete-my-data. We will ask Google Play for the order identifier needed to locate the record, delete the RevenueCat customer record, and confirm. Note that Google keeps its own transaction records independently of us, for tax and refund purposes.

Children

Mononote is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from a child. There is nothing in the app to sign up for and nothing to share with anyone.

Security, stated honestly

Encryption at rest means that a copy of your phone's storage, a stolen backup or a disk image cannot be read without the key, and the key never leaves the device. It does not protect against someone using your unlocked phone, against malware running with the app's own permissions, or against anyone who can see your lock screen or your home-screen widget. We do not claim end-to-end encryption, because there is no second end; there is no server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and a material change is announced in the app's release notes.
