We love plants, right?

So how many do you have at home — 20, 50, more than 100? It can get hard to keep track. Which one has been watered? Was this Ficus fertilised lately? Planter keeps all of those answers in a single place.

It has a diary and a calendar view to help you browse and track any plant-related events and actions, and your plant care is fully customisable: adjust properties like sunlight intensity, pruning, misting and fertilising at any time.

Planter is designed to be minimalistic and intuitive, and it works on both Android and iOS.
