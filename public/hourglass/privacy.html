Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Applies to Hourglass (Clepsydra) 3.3 and later on Android and iOS

The short version. Hourglass has no accounts and keeps your timers and settings on your device. It is free because it shows one small banner ad, and AppLovin's ad SDK - not us - collects the data needed to serve that ad, including your device's advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad loads, and you can change that choice at any time from the app's settings. One optional purchase removes the ad for good; RevenueCat checks the store receipt for us and we never see your payment details. The app also sends anonymous usage events to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which features get used.

1. Who we are

Hourglass (also referred to by its project name, Clepsydra) is published by Michał Daniel Dobrzański ("we", "us"). Contact: dobrzanskioscillator@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device only

To function as a timer, the app stores a small amount of data locally (Android: app preferences; iOS: user defaults and the widget App Group):

  • your chosen timer duration, saved timers and sequences, and the timer's current state (including the time it is due to finish);
  • your preferences: sand color theme, sound, vibration, gravity mode, zen mode, language and keep-screen-awake settings;
  • your answer to the app's personalized-ads question (section 3).

We never receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app (on Android it may be included in your device's standard encrypted backups, controlled by your device settings).

3. Advertising - the data that leaves your device

The app shows a single banner ad at the bottom of the timer screen, served by AppLovin MAX (AppLovin Corporation, 1100 Page Mill Road, Palo Alto, CA 94304, USA) through the AppLovin SDK embedded in the app. It is the only advertising library in the app, and it is never started for users who bought the ad-free unlock (section 5).

When an ad is requested, the SDK sends AppLovin information that typically includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA where you have allowed tracking);
  • your IP address, and the approximate location AppLovin derives from it;
  • device and app information: model, operating-system version, language, screen size, the app's identifier and version;
  • ad-interaction data: which ads were shown, whether one was tapped, and how long it was visible.

AppLovin uses this to select and cap ads, to measure them, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud. Where you have consented (or where personalized advertising is lawful without consent), the data may also be used to build an advertising profile and personalize ads across apps and sites. AppLovin acts as an independent controller for that processing under its own terms - see the AppLovin privacy policy. MAX is a mediation platform; in the current release no additional ad networks are enabled, so AppLovin is the only recipient of the data above. If we add networks later we will update this policy and the stores' privacy declarations first.

We ourselves receive only aggregate, non-identifying reporting from AppLovin (impressions and revenue per country). We do not receive your advertising ID, we cannot identify you from those reports, we do not sell personal data and we use no attribution service.

Your legal basis and your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks you, before the first ad loads, whether it may use your advertising identifier to show personalized ads. The legal basis for personalized advertising and for storing and accessing information on your device is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules). If you choose basic ads instead, the app still works in full and AppLovin is told not to personalize. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through Privacy options in the app's settings; withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened.

On iOS the system additionally asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not available to the SDK and you see non-personalized ads. You can revisit this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland, personalized advertising is based on our and AppLovin's legitimate interest in funding a free app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR where it applies). You can still limit it with your device controls: Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset the advertising ID, and iOS lets you deny tracking as described above. Privacy options in the app's settings also lets you switch to basic ads anywhere in the world, which we pass on to AppLovin as a "do not sell or share" signal. Buying the ad-free unlock ends all of this processing for your device.

4. Usage analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data that helps us understand how the app is used and improve it. This covers app-interaction events - for example: a timer was started, paused, finished or reset; a duration was set; a setting was changed (theme, sound, vibration, gravity mode, zen mode, language); the ad-free unlock was offered, bought or restored; an ad impression was paid. Alongside these, Firebase automatically collects a randomly generated app-instance identifier and basic technical context (device model, operating-system and app version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address by Google).

We use it for exactly two things: understanding which features get used, and understanding how much revenue the ads produce so we can keep the app free. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location, we do not use analytics to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share this data. Firebase Analytics acts as our processor and Google processes the data under its own terms (see Firebase privacy and Google's Privacy Policy). This processing is in our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app.

5. The ad-free unlock

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase that removes the banner ad permanently and unlocks all sand colors. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies; we never see your payment details. To know whether you own the unlock, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), which validates the store receipt and stores an anonymous, app-generated identifier together with your purchase history for this app. RevenueCat acts as our processor - see its privacy policy. The unlock is tied to your store account and can be restored on any device signed in to it via Restore purchases in the app's settings.

6. Permissions and system features we use, and why

Permission / featureWhy the app needs it
Notifications (Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS, iOS notification authorization) To alert you when the timer finishes while the app is in the background. Optional - the timer still works if you decline, but you will only see the alert inside the app.
Exact alarms (Android USE_EXACT_ALARM) So the finished-timer alert fires at the exact moment your countdown ends, even in battery-saving modes. This permission exists specifically for alarm and timer apps.
Run at boot (Android RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) To re-arm a running timer's alarm if your device restarts before the timer ends. The app does not otherwise run at startup.
Vibration (Android VIBRATE, iOS haptics) For the vibration alert when the timer finishes (can be turned off in settings).
Advertising ID (Android com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), App Tracking Transparency (iOS) Used only by the ad SDK, as described in section 3. Never requested for users who own the ad-free unlock.
Internet access To load ads, verify the ad-free unlock and send the analytics events above. The timer itself works offline.
Time-sensitive notifications, Live Activities (iOS) So the finished-timer alert can break through Focus modes, and so the running countdown can appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

The app uses no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The ad SDK is configured for a general audience; if you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account and no identity for you, the effective controls are on your device: withdraw ad consent in the app's settings, reset or delete your advertising ID, deny tracking on iOS, or uninstall the app, which removes all on-device data. For data held by AppLovin, its privacy policy explains how to exercise your rights with them. If you have questions about your rights under GDPR, CCPA or similar laws, contact us at the address below; you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.
