Oscillator

Games

Ten classic arcade & puzzle games. Free, offline, in 14 languages.

Quick sessions, no accounts, no waiting: open a game, play a round in under a minute, beat your best. Every game is available for Android and iOS, and each one is localized into 14 languages you can switch inside the app.

Dino Jump

Jump the cacti, duck the birds. How far can your dino run?

Saper

The classic mine puzzle: logic, flags and chording. Three difficulties.

2048 Merge

Swipe, merge, reach 2048 — then keep going.

Snake Classic

The snake you remember: eat, grow, don't bite yourself.

Flappy Wings

One tap to flap. Thread the pipes. Beat your best.

Brick Breaker

Smash the wall, keep the ball alive, climb the levels.

Memory Match

Flip cards and find every pair in the fewest moves.

Color Memory

Watch the colors. Repeat the pattern. How long can you last?

Sudoku Classic

Clean sudoku with fair puzzles — every board has one solution.

15 Puzzle

Slide the tiles into order. Every shuffle is solvable.
